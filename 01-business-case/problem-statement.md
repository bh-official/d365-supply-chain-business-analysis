# Business Case: Supply Chain Process Improvement

## 1. Background

This case study considers a fictional manufacturing company that purchases components from international suppliers, stores materials in warehouses and delivers finished products to customers.

The company wants to improve visibility across purchasing, inventory management, supplier invoicing and customer deliveries using an integrated ERP system such as Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations.

## 2. Problem Statement

The company has identified several operational challenges:

- Purchase order status is not consistently visible to purchasing and warehouse teams.
- Differences between ordered quantities and received quantities are not always identified promptly.
- Inventory records do not always match the physical stock available in the warehouse.
- Supplier invoices sometimes contain quantities that differ from recorded goods receipts.
- Delivery delays are not consistently highlighted to the teams responsible for customer fulfilment.

These issues can result in additional manual work, delayed payments, inaccurate reporting and reduced customer satisfaction.

## 3. Business Objectives

The proposed improvement aims to:

1. Improve visibility of purchase orders and expected deliveries.
2. Increase inventory accuracy and stock traceability.
3. Identify supplier invoice discrepancies before payment approval.
4. Improve communication between purchasing, warehouse and finance teams.
5. Establish meaningful performance indicators for supply chain operations.

## 4. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Main responsibility |
|---|---|
| Procurement team | Purchase orders and supplier communication |
| Warehouse team | Goods receipt and inventory accuracy |
| Finance team | Supplier invoices and payment controls |
| Sales team | Customer orders and delivery commitments |
| Supply Chain Manager | Operational performance and process improvement |
| IT and ERP team | System requirements, configuration and support |

## 5. Scope

### In scope

- Purchase order processing
- Goods receipt and quantity discrepancies
- Inventory visibility
- Supplier invoice matching
- Delivery status reporting
- Business requirements and user acceptance testing

### Out of scope

- Actual implementation or configuration of Dynamics 365
- Live integration with supplier systems
- Real financial transactions
- Production deployment

## 6. Proposed Approach

The Business Analyst will document the current process, gather stakeholder requirements, identify gaps and propose an improved process.

The proposed solution will be assessed against agreed business requirements and validated through user acceptance testing scenarios.

## 7. Success Measures

The company will establish baseline measurements before setting improvement targets for:

- Inventory record accuracy
- Purchase order processing time
- Supplier invoice discrepancy rate
- On-time delivery rate
- Time taken to resolve operational exceptions

Targets will be agreed with stakeholders after reviewing the available data.

## 8. Assumptions

This is a fictional case study. The problems described are illustrative and have not been measured in a real organisation.

The proposed use of Dynamics 365 represents a potential solution to be assessed, not a confirmed system implementation.

## 9. Expected Business Benefits

The proposed improvements could reduce manual reconciliation, improve operational visibility, strengthen financial controls and support better decision-making across the supply chain.
