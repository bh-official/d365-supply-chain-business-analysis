# Supply Chain Business Analysis Portfolio

## Project Overview

This portfolio demonstrates my developing Business Analysis skills through a fictional supply chain improvement project for a manufacturing company.

The project explores how business analysis techniques can help improve procurement, goods receipt, inventory accuracy and supplier invoice processing in an ERP environment, with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations as the reference platform.

## Business Problem

The fictional company experiences challenges with purchase order visibility, partial deliveries, inventory record accuracy, supplier invoice discrepancies and operational reporting.

The project explores how these problems can be investigated, documented and translated into clear business requirements and measurable improvement opportunities.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse current and proposed business processes.
- Identify operational gaps and potential improvements.
- Gather and document business requirements.
- Develop user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Prioritise requirements using MoSCoW.
- Assess change impacts, risks and issues.
- Design UAT scenarios and maintain requirements traceability.
- Define supply chain KPIs to measure business outcomes.
- Develop a proposed implementation roadmap.

## ERP Learning Focus

The project uses Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations as its reference platform, focusing on supply chain management concepts such as purchase orders, goods receipts, inventory records, supplier invoice matching and operational reporting.

The proposed capabilities are learning examples. They have not been configured, implemented or tested in a live Dynamics 365 environment.

## Project Status
