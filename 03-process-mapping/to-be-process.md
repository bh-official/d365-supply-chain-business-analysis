# To-Be Process: Improved Procurement and Goods Receipt

## 1. Objective

Design an improved procurement and goods receipt process that provides better visibility of purchase orders, improves inventory accuracy and helps finance identify supplier invoice discrepancies before payment approval.

## 2. Proposed Process

1. The procurement team records the material requirement and creates a purchase order.
2. The purchase order is communicated to the supplier and made available to authorised users.
3. The warehouse team reviews expected deliveries against open purchase orders.
4. On arrival, the warehouse team checks the delivered items and quantities.
5. The actual quantity received is recorded against the relevant purchase order.
6. Inventory records are updated according to the approved goods receipt process.
7. The finance team matches the supplier invoice against the purchase order and recorded goods receipt.
8. Any quantity or price discrepancies are flagged for investigation.
9. An authorised person resolves the discrepancy according to company policy.
10. The invoice proceeds through the normal approval and payment process once the required checks are satisfied.

## 3. Proposed Improvements

- Improve visibility of open purchase orders and expected deliveries.
- Record actual quantities received rather than assuming the full order has arrived.
- Establish clear ownership for investigating discrepancies.
- Define approval rules and tolerances with finance stakeholders.
- Provide reports showing outstanding purchase orders, unresolved discrepancies and overdue deliveries.

## 4. Potential Dynamics 365 Capabilities

The proposed workflow could be supported by relevant Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management and Finance capabilities, including purchase order management, product receipt recording, inventory management and vendor invoice matching.

The precise configuration and controls would need to be validated against business requirements.

## 5. Business Analyst Responsibilities

The Business Analyst would:

- Validate the proposed process with procurement, warehouse and finance stakeholders.
- Document functional and non-functional requirements.
- Identify gaps between the current process and the required outcome.
- Work with functional consultants to assess system capabilities.
- Define acceptance criteria and prepare user acceptance tests.
- Support defect resolution and obtain business sign-off.

## 6. Expected Benefits

- Improved visibility of purchasing and receiving activities.
- Better inventory traceability.
- Earlier identification of invoice discrepancies.
- Clearer accountability for resolving exceptions.
- More reliable operational reporting.

## 7. Dependencies and Risks

- Accurate product, supplier and purchasing data.
- Appropriate user permissions and approval controls.
- Agreement on invoice matching tolerances.
- Training for procurement, warehouse and finance users.
- Stakeholder approval of the proposed workflow.

## 8. Validation

The proposed process should be reviewed with stakeholders and tested using representative scenarios before any production implementation.
